# FiftyThree

FiftyThree, Inc. was a New York City technology company known for Paper (2012 iPad App of the Year), the Pencil Bluetooth stylus, and Paste. It raised a Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) in 2013 and shipped the FiftyThree SDK for Pencil in 2014. FiftyThree's assets and patents were acquired by WeTransfer on August 21, 2018, and its products and developer surface were wound down.

Status: defunct (no live public web API, developer portal, or OpenAPI). This profile is a historical company record.
